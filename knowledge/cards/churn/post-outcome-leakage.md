**Claim.** "Called retention line", "cancellation form opened", "last invoice unpaid after termination", "zero usage in the last 7 days when the label is 30-day inactivity" are consequences of churning, not predictors. A feature with suspicious importance and a timestamp near the label is a leak until proven otherwise.

**How to apply.** Reviewer asks for the point-in-time proof of every top-10 feature.
